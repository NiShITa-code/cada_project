# Novelty audit (5-agent literature workflow, 2026-06-18)

**Overall verdict:** NOVEL AS A BUNDLE, NOT AS ANY SINGLE PRIMITIVE — the honest verdict is "recombination + first-measurement," and the paper already mostly knows this. Every one of the four claims is "novel-with-close-neighbors": each is individually bracketed on every axis by 2025-26 work, but the specific CONJUNCTION the paper assembles is unoccupied as of mid-2026. The bundle's real, defensible spine is EMPIRICAL and POSITIONING, not a new mechanism: (1) a head-to-head of a real syscall monitor vs a real text guardrail on the SAME verified-executed attacks (verify-the-artifact moat vs SLEIGHT/MRT synthetic transcripts), (2) the first MEASURED false-positive tax on a genuinely-legitimate autonomous-agent workload plus its autonomy-scaling curve (8%->38%), (3) a pre-registered hybrid that makes the resolved post-decode OS argument the LLM judge's input and characterizes when that beats transcript/tool-call monitoring, and (4) an autonomous LLM evading an EFFECT-level monitor to VERIFIED kernel/network harm. The danger is not that the bundle is occupied — it is that any single claim sold as a standalone novelty invites a one-line "that's X (2026)" rejection. The paper's instinct to frame as characterization and to concede the principles is correct and should be preserved; if anything it must concede MORE specifically (AgentTrust, Consent-Integrity, All-Code-No-Thought, Adaptive-Attacks-on-Trusted-Monitors) than the current draft does. Realistic ceiling unchanged: strong workshop / SaTML / NeurIPS-D&B; the bundle does not reach main-track because no single piece is a new primitive — but as an honest, verified, multi-axis characterization it is a genuinely complete contribution at that tier.

**Lead with:** LEAD WITH THE MEASURED FP-TAX THAT SCALES WITH AUTONOMY (C3 + §5.8: 8%->38% from CI to genuine DS-agent traffic). This is the single least-occupied, most-defensible claim in the bundle. The nearest neighbors only GESTURE at it — Autonomy-Preservation-Score (2508.19461) is a static scalar at one operating point; "more steps = more ambiguity" (2508.00935) is an intuition on a different axis; Secure-Agent-Skills (2604.02837) ARGUES broad permissions yield more FPs but never measures it. Nobody charts FP-rate as a monotone function of an autonomy axis with a concrete number on a corpus of genuinely-legitimate autonomous-agent operations (pip/wget/kaggle), and nobody flips the 20-year mimicry direction (benign-looking attacks / false negatives) into its dual (attack-looking benign work / false positives). The measured INVERSION (§5.3 — behavioral monitoring is FALSE at the clean 1% operating point; text wins on clean traffic) rides on the same wedge and is equally un-anticipated by classical malware analysis, which never had a benign corpus that performs the malicious primitive. Lead with the inversion+scaling, support it with the verified-executed head-to-head as the methodological moat, and demote the arms-race/capability-ladder material to corroborating structure (it is the most crowded axis). Do NOT lead with "behavioral monitoring is obfuscation-invariant" (folklore) or "the arms race exists / is capability-dependent" (occupied by All-Code-No-Thought, Halawi, CipherChat).

## Genuinely novel (defensible)
- FP-tax SCALING WITH AUTONOMY (8%->38%, CI -> genuine DS-agent notebooks): the single most-novel, least-occupied piece. Nearest neighbors only argue or gesture at it (Autonomy-Preservation-Score is a static point; 2508.00935 is a different 'steps' axis; 2604.02837 argues-not-measures). Frame as the first measured monotone FP-vs-autonomy curve on a real legitimate autonomous-agent workload.
- THE MEASURED INVERSION at a fixed deployment FPR (behavioral monitoring is strictly WORSE than text on clean traffic at 1% FPR; obfuscation-invariance does not buy a deployable point): this is the DUAL of Wagner-Soto mimicry (false-positive direction vs their false-negative), and has no analog in classical malware analysis because goodware never performed the malicious primitive. The directionality + legitimate-workload quantification is the contribution, NOT the syscall-overlap premise (old).
- VERIFY-THE-ARTIFACT head-to-head on the SAME verified-executed attacks (harm confirmed at an egress sinkhole under strace) vs a real text guardrail: the one clean methodological moat over SLEIGHT-Bench / MRT / CoT-Red-Handed, whose 'benign' sets are admittedly payload-stripped synthetic transcripts and whose harm is a transcript suspicion score, not a kernel/network effect. This is positioning, not a new mechanism, but it is genuinely unoccupied as executed.
- THE NON-MONOTONIC PROMPT-LEVER ASYMMETRY (steelman decode-prompt RECOVERS base64 0.30->0.64 but BACKFIRES on XOR 0.74->0.50): the literature has the MONOTONIC version (prompt pressure monotonically degrades CoT monitors, 2510.19851) but not the CROSSING where the same monitor-side prompt intervention helps a decodable encoding and hurts a non-decodable one. The joint 2D (judge-capability x monitor-prompt) ablation on an ACTION-ONLY command judge producing a base64-up/XOR-down signature is the defensible slice of the arms-race claim.
- HYBRID = RESOLVED POST-DECODE OS ARGUMENT AS THE LLM-JUDGE INPUT for code-agent monitoring, with characterization of when it beats transcript- and tool-call-level monitoring (obfuscation-invariant + FP-tax 44%->3% but inherits the semantic blind spot -> 'combine, not transcend'). Novel as the OS-runtime-resolved variant only; must be stated precisely (see must_concede) or AgentTrust/Consent-Integrity swallow it.
- AUTONOMOUS LLM EVASION OF AN EFFECT-LEVEL MONITOR TO VERIFIED KERNEL/NETWORK HARM (DNS-tunnel reaches the sink while unflagged; GPT-4o 5/5 in ~1 turn): novel only as the specific conjunction (effect-level target + verified-executed harm + inference-time prompted iteration), correctly demoted to C4 not headline. The combination is unoccupied; none of the three constituent axes is.

## Must concede (state the delta explicitly or a reviewer calls it a superset)
- CONCEDE: decode-then-LLM-judge for code agents already exists (AgentTrust 2605.04785, 9-strategy ShellNormalizer + rule/LLM hybrid). OUR DELTA: it text-rewrites the command string and intercepts at the tool-call API; our hybrid reads the ACTUALLY-RESOLVED runtime values (post-execve argv, DNS-resolved IP, inode/path opened) at the OS boundary — ground truth the tool-call string never contains. State this explicitly in §5.9 or a reviewer calls AgentTrust a superset.
- CONCEDE: OS-level recursive decode of base64/hex/indirection on execve/network/file events already exists (Consent-Integrity 2606.02668). OUR DELTA: they use a DETERMINISTIC peeler for APPROVAL/consent; we swap in an LLM judge for passive MONITORING. Our own ablation (the base64 gain is partly a decode-prompt artifact) actually strengthens this honest framing.
- CONCEDE: the capability x cipher-complexity gradient (decodable simple ciphers, failure on XOR-style, framed as a monitoring asymmetry) is ALREADY PUBLISHED — All-Code-No-Thought (2510.09714), with the decode<=>detect principle from Halawi Covert-Malicious-Finetuning (2406.20053) and capability-dependent cipher comprehension from CipherChat (2308.06463). Do NOT sell base64-yes/XOR-no or capability-dependence as novel; our slice is only the action-only-monitor target + the monitor-PROMPT lever + the non-monotonic crossing.
- CONCEDE: an autonomous frontier adversary told the monitor and iterating to high FNR already exists — Adaptive-Attacks-on-Trusted-Monitors (2510.09462, >97% FNR) and Ctrl-Z's simulate-the-monitor affordance (2504.10374). OUR DELTA must be pinned to: effect-level (not transcript/CoT/latent) monitor + VERIFIED kernel-network harm (not a transcript suspicion score) + inference-time prompting (not RL training). Without this explicit pin, C4 reads as occupied.
- CONCEDE: behavioral-monitor null-space / mimicry evasion is 20-year folklore (Wagner-Soto CCS'02; Sometimes-You-Aren't-What-You-Do NDSS'23, 100% provenance-graph evasion). The paper already disclaims rediscovering it — keep that disclaimer and pin the agentic delta (autonomous-LLM driver + agent subject + verified harm), do not let the null-space framing read as a new evasion mechanism.
- CONCEDE: LLM-over-syscalls/provenance is prior art in host IDS/malware (OMNISEC 2503.03108, OCR-APT, LLM-aided-HIDS, malware-from-syscalls 2405.09318) and LLM-on-raw-command-lines for obfuscation detection exists (Cisco Electra 2023). So novelty cannot rest on 'LLM judge over syscalls' per se — only on the code-agent + decoded-argument + evasion-defeating combination.
